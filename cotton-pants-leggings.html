WHOLESALE FAQ

Common buyer questions.

Use these points as a quick guide. Exact commercial and production details are confirmed against each enquiry.

Can I request a mixed collection?

Yes. Mixed styles and colour programmes can be discussed as part of the product brief.

Can specifications be customised?

Fabric feel, stretch, colours, sizing, waistband, pocket details, stitching and finishing can be discussed to requirement.

Can private-label requirements be discussed?

Private-label discussions are available where required and can be included in the initial enquiry.

How are final prices and quantities confirmed?

Pricing, availability, minimum quantities and production details are confirmed after the requirement has been reviewed.

Can I share a reference or detailed brief?

Yes. Use the requirement field to describe your reference, fit, fabric, packaging or other sourcing needs.

What should I include in my first enquiry?

Company or buyer name, target market, product, estimated quantity, size range, preferred colours and any special requirements.

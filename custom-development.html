DEVELOPMENT PROCESS

A clear path from idea to production.

A focused development workflow for buyers who need a textile product shaped around a specific commercial or project requirement.

01 · BRIEF

Share the Direction

Send a reference image, existing sample, sketch or product idea together with the intended use and key requirements.

02 · DEVELOP

Shape the Product

Discuss dimensions, colour, material, construction, finishing and other details needed to bring the requirement into a workable product direction.

03 · PRODUCE

Prepare for Supply

Once the development direction is agreed, production, quantities, packing and project requirements can be discussed for the intended order.

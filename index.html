Wholesale Information

Clear Terms for Professional Wholesale Buying.

Designed for wholesalers, importers, retailers and private-label buyers who need clear ordering, quality and dispatch expectations.

Minimum Order

Wholesale MOQ is 50 PCS. Final quantity, assortment and pricing are confirmed on the quotation.

Pricing & Quotes

Prices depend on product, quality, quantity, specifications and destination. A written quotation is required before order confirmation.

Quality & Approval

Product details, measurements, materials and finish should be confirmed before production. Buyers may request available samples where applicable.

Order Confirmation

Orders enter production or packing only after specifications, quantities, pricing and payment terms are mutually confirmed.

Production & Lead Time

Lead time varies by collection, quantity, customization and stock status. The confirmed quotation or order confirmation will state the applicable timeline.

Shipping

Shipping method, freight responsibility, destination charges and export documentation are agreed before dispatch. Transit time depends on the selected carrier and destination.

Sample Policy

Samples can be arranged where applicable to confirm product quality, specifications and finish before bulk production. Sample availability, charges, shipping and approval requirements are agreed with the buyer in advance.

Custom & Private Label

Custom colours, specifications, packaging and private-label requirements can be discussed subject to quantity, feasibility and written approval.

Buyer note: Product images may show styling or presentation examples. Final specifications, available options, commercial terms and delivery commitments are those confirmed in the buyer’s quotation or order confirmation.

Wholesale FAQ

Questions Before You Source

A concise guide for buyers planning their next textile programme.

Can I source products beyond the displayed range?

Yes. Share the category, reference, material, finish or intended use, and the team can review the sourcing or development requirement.

Can several product categories be combined?

Yes. Buyers can submit a consolidated requirement across collections, with quantities and specifications reviewed for each product.

Can you develop for a specific market?

Market-led colours, dimensions, fabrics, patterns and presentation details can be explored for suitable wholesale programmes.

What should I include in my first enquiry?

Product preferences, approximate quantities, quality level, destination and desired timeline help us prepare a focused sourcing response.
